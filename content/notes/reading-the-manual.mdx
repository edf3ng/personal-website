Every tool I have gotten genuinely good at, I got good at by reading its
documentation front to back once, early, before I needed any of it.

This feels like a waste of an afternoon and is not. The point is not retention.
The point is building an index: you will not remember how `damp3` smooths a
vector, but you will remember that something in that library does, and that is
the expensive half of the lookup.

Search results optimise for the question you already know how to ask. Manuals
are the only place that tells you what you should have been asking.

> The fastest way to stop fighting a tool is to find out what it thinks it is
> for.

Press <KeyCap>?</KeyCap> in most good software and you will find somebody already
wrote this down for you.

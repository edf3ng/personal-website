You sit down after dark. Chicago rain on the glass, a warm window in the
middle of the screen. The folders on the right are the whole site.

## How it fits together

The desktop lives in the root layout. Routing only changes which window is
open. Close it and you are back at the rain.

Icons come from the Tango Desktop Project. The wallpaper is a 2008 photograph
by Daniel Powell, used under CC BY 2.0. Nunito and Lora keep the chrome
soft instead of corporate.

## The desktop is the nav

There is no header of links. About, projects, notes, and resume are icons.
Click one and a window appears. Drag it if you want it out of the way.
